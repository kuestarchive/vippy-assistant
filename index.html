          About

          Vippy is a free, open-source, voice-first travel assistant proof of concept. It uses your browser's built-in speech recognition and speech synthesis, OpenStreetMap for maps, Nominatim for place search, and OSRM for walking directions — all free and open, no API keys or Google Maps required.
